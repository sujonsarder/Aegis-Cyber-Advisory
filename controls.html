Recommended Information System Security Controls

Administrative, technical and physical controls mapped to the STRIDE threats and NIST CSF 2.0 gaps identified for Lifeline Medical Centre.

Physical Controls

Safeguards for facilities where clinical systems and records physically reside.

Physical Access Restrictions

Data centre, network rooms and records room restricted to authorized staff; locked racks; clean-desk and screen-lock rules at nursing stations.

Biometric / Smart Card Access

Smart card or fingerprint entry for data centre with access logs; smart-card tap-in on shared clinical workstations.

CCTV Surveillance

Cameras on data centre, entrances and server corridors; footage retained 90 days and reviewed after incidents.

Visitor Management

Visitor registration, photo ID, badges and escorts for vendors and contractors in sensitive areas.

Environmental Controls

Fire detection and clean-agent suppression, UPS and generator with scheduled tests, temperature and water leak sensors.

Media & Device Protection

Secure disposal of drives and paper records, asset tags, offsite storage for backup media.

How the Controls Reduce Risk

Against MTMT Threats

  • Spoofing (T01–T03): MFA, vendor access governance, training.
  • Tampering (T04–T06): WAF and secure coding, TLS and hashing, immutable backups.
  • Repudiation (T07–T09): SIEM logging, unique IDs, AUP.
  • Information Disclosure (T10–T12): RBAC, encryption, DLP.
  • Denial of Service (T13–T15): DDoS protection, DR, UPS, segmentation.
  • Elevation of Privilege (T16–T18): patching, PAM, IoMT isolation.

Improvement to NIST CSF 2.0 Posture

  • Govern: policies, vendor management and risk practice move from Tier 1 to 3.
  • Identify: asset register and vulnerability management create visibility.
  • Protect: MFA, RBAC, encryption and segmentation protect the Crown Jewels.
  • Detect: SIEM and EDR cut detection time from weeks to hours.
  • Respond / Recover: IR plan, immutable backups and DR testing restore operations quickly.
