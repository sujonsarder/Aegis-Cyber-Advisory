Organization Overview

Lifeline Medical Centre (LMC) is a hypothetical private tertiary-care hospital in Dhaka providing inpatient, outpatient, emergency, diagnostic and pharmacy services.

  • Laboratory and radiology diagnostics
  • In-house pharmacy

Major Information Systems

  • HIS / EMR: patient registration, clinical notes, orders, discharge

💎 Crown Jewels

The assets whose loss, corruption or unavailability would cause the greatest harm to patients, operations and reputation.

1. Electronic Patient Records (EMR)

Identity, diagnoses, treatment and history. Subject to privacy obligations and essential for safe care.

2. HIS/EMR & Clinical Systems

Availability of HIS, LIS and PACS directly affects patient safety; downtime forces paper-based, error-prone care.

3. Diagnostic Results & Imaging

Integrity of lab values and images is vital; tampering could lead to wrong treatment.

4. Prescription & Pharmacy Data

Incorrect or altered medication data creates direct patient safety risk.

5. Patient Financial & Payment Data

Billing, insurance and payment details attract fraud and criminal interest.

6. Privileged Credentials & Backups

Admin accounts and backup repositories; their compromise enables total system takeover and prevents recovery.
