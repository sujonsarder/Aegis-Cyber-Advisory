Security Improvement Roadmap

A 24-month, risk-prioritized plan that closes the most dangerous gaps first and builds toward full NIST CSF 2.0 alignment.

Phased Timeline

Priorities are set by business risk: threats that endanger patient safety and Crown Jewels come first.

Messages for Management

1. Act now on identity and backup

MFA and isolated backups are low-cost steps that remove the likeliest routes to a hospital-wide outage.

2. Protect the Crown Jewels first

Spending follows patient safety and data sensitivity: EMR, clinical systems, credentials and backups.

3. Govern and measure

Appoint ownership, report quarterly and track CSF maturity so investment shows measurable risk reduction.

Feasibility note: Short-term actions rely largely on existing licences and staff; medium and long-term items need budget approval and may use phased procurement or managed services. Cost figures should be added after vendor quotations.
