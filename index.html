Our Consulting Methodology

Every engagement follows a seven-step approach, aligned with ISO/IEC 27005 risk thinking, NIST CSF 2.0 and Microsoft's STRIDE threat modeling method:

  • 1. Understand the business, its processes and information systems.
  • 2. Identify information assets and the "Crown Jewels".
  • 3. Model threats with the Microsoft Threat Modeling Tool (MTMT) and STRIDE.
  • 4. Assess posture against the six NIST CSF 2.0 functions.
  • 5. Design administrative, technical and physical controls.
  • 6. Prioritize an improvement roadmap by business risk.
  • 7. Communicate findings to management in plain language.

Client Engagement Summary

Current engagement: Lifeline Medical Centre (LMC), a hypothetical 250-bed private hospital in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Engagement scope: Hospital Information System (HIS/EMR), laboratory and radiology systems, pharmacy, billing and payment, patient portal and mobile app, and supporting infrastructure.
Headline finding: Patient records and clinical systems are the organization's Crown Jewels, yet MFA, centralized logging and tested recovery are absent. These gaps drive the high-priority actions in the roadmap.

Key Risk

Ransomware and credential theft against clinical systems could halt patient care and expose sensitive health data.

Overall Posture

Average NIST CSF 2.0 maturity of roughly 1.5 out of 4: foundational practices exist but are informal.

Recommended Path

Quick wins in 0–6 months (MFA, backups, policy), structural controls in 6–12, full alignment in 12–24.

Disclaimer: Aegis Cyber Advisory and Lifeline Medical Centre are fictional entities created for an academic exercise (MPMIS 516, University of Dhaka). Findings are illustrative.
